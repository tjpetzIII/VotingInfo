# VOT-67 Mail ballot journey

## User Story 1 (P1)
As a voter, I can choose a state and follow official steps to request, return, track, or resolve a mail ballot without entering credentials or receiving an acceptance claim.

Requirements: provide typed official resources for all 50 states and DC with explicit local/unknown status, preserve election/address context, show stale limitations, use safe external links, and provide bilingual accessible copy with deterministic tests.
